What this view shows

    Tip: click any bar, season, zone, hexagon or table row to filter to it. Undo walks it back.

    Where the shots come from on the floor

    Each hexagon is a patch of court. Bigger = more of the selected shots came from there. Color = points per shot from that spot (blue = poor, red = good). Hover for details.

    Data: NBA Stats API regular-season shot logs, via NBA_Shots_04_25. The dashboard loads data/processed/cube.csv (shots summed by season × team × court zone × home/away), games.csv, teams.csv, and bins.csv + bins_key.json (shot locations), and does every calculation in the browser. Free throws are not in the data. Definitions are in the report’s About the data section.
